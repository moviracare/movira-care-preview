Where we began

Why we work this way

In the usual arrangement, a family pays an agency once and is then left alone with everything else — salary, leave, absences, disputes. We would rather stay responsible. When we employ the staff, we have every reason to choose carefully, pay on time, and fix problems quickly.

— What we do: recruit, check, train, employ and supervise.

Our values

What we stand for

Care with Love, Trust & Comfort.

  • Love

    Care given with patience and dignity — the way you’d want it for your own parents.

  • Trust

    Checks before anyone starts, written agreements, honesty about what we can and can’t do.

  • Comfort

    For the person receiving care, for the family, and for our staff.
