Our story

Our story

Why we started.

When it works, finding help through word of mouth is luck. When it doesn’t, the family starts again, often in the middle of a crisis, and the worker moves on with nothing to show for it.

Movira Care was started to change who is responsible. We don’t pass on phone numbers. We choose people, check them, train them and register them with us. We pay them on time and treat them fairly, because people who are treated well do better work and stay longer. We put every arrangement in writing. And while someone of ours is working in your home, we answer for them.

That costs more than hiring someone directly, and we say so. What the difference pays for is simple: you are never left managing a stranger on your own.

Where we began

A note from our founder

Help at home, done properly.

When I looked at how families in Jammu found help at home, I saw how unorganised and unprofessional it was. Workers were sent from house to house, someone took a cut, and nobody stayed responsible: not for the family, and not for the worker.

I started Movira Care on 23 May 2026 to do it properly. We check every person we send, train them, supervise them and pay them on time. Families get one team to call, and our staff never pay us a rupee.

Manish Kotwal, Founder, Movira Care

Our philosophy

Our philosophy

What we believe.

  • A home isn’t like any other workplace

    Whoever walks through the door is trusted with a family’s private life.

  • Finding someone is easy

    Answering for them is the real work.

  • Good care starts with how the caretaker is treated

    Fairness to staff is how we protect families.

  • What’s written down can be relied on

    Tasks, hours, charges, the daily log, a complaint: in writing.

  • The person being helped stays in charge

    Help fits around their life, not the other way round.

  • Honesty about limits is part of care

    We say what we don’t do, and when you need a nurse or a doctor instead.

Our values

Our values

Our values, and how you can see them.

  • We answer for our people

    Checked, paid and supervised by us; one office to call; a replacement if they leave.

  • Fair work, always

    ₹0 fees from staff; pay by the 5th; rest and a weekly off.

  • Write it down

    A written agreement, a daily log, one invoice.

  • Tell the truth

    Clear limits on every service; checks reduce risk but can’t remove it.

  • Dignity in every home

    Your routine and language respected, and every staff member treated as a professional.
