Questions families ask us.

Straight answers, taken from the agreement we sign with you. Can’t find yours? Call or WhatsApp us.

Answers

21 questions

Who employs the staff member?

Movira Care. We pay their salary and are responsible for their statutory dues, leave and conduct. You give day-to-day instructions within the agreed tasks.

Can I choose a female or male caretaker?

Yes. Tell us your preference when you first contact us.

Can I meet the staff member first?

Yes. We share their name, photo and ID card number, and you can meet them at our office in Channi Himmat.

Which languages do your staff speak?

Tell us the language spoken at home and we will match where possible.

What is the difference between a caretaker and a nurse?

A caretaker helps with daily living: hygiene, feeding, moving safely, medicine reminders from the label, and company. A registered nurse does clinical tasks like injections, dressings and catheter care — only on a doctor’s written instruction.

How do I pay?

One monthly invoice, paid in advance, by cash, UPI or bank transfer. Part months are charged pro-rata.

Should I pay the staff member directly?

No — please pay only Movira Care, and don’t give wages, advances or loans to staff. Small festival gifts are fine.

Is the registration fee refundable?

Yes, in full, if we can’t offer a suitable match within 10 days, or if you cancel before we share a profile. Once you accept a match the fee isn’t refundable — our replacement promise applies instead.

What if I pay late?

If an invoice is unpaid 3 days after the due date, we may suspend service after giving 3 days’ written notice. We will never leave a patient or child alone without arranging a safe handover with you.

What is the standard duty?

8 hours a day, with one weekly off. Longer duty is overtime, billed at twice the hourly rate.

What happens on the weekly off?

We can send relief staff for the day at the relief rate. If you don’t want relief, the staff member takes the day off.

What do live-in staff need?

A private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

What if the staff member doesn’t come?

We aim to send a replacement the same day for 24-hour or critical care, and within 24 hours otherwise. You’re not charged for days no one came.

What if we’re not comfortable with the person?

In the first 7 days we’ll replace them free, for any reason. After that, free replacement applies if they resign, if we withdraw them, or if they’re absent more than 2 days in a row. Replacement depends on availability.

How do I stop the service?

Give 7 days’ written notice (or pay 7 days in lieu). Any unused prepaid amount is refunded within 7 working days.

Can I hire the staff member directly later?

Within 12 months after the service ends, only with our written consent; otherwise a fee of one month’s service charge applies. This covers our recruitment and training costs.

Can I have CCTV at home?

Yes, in shared areas — please tell us so we can inform staff before they start. No cameras in washrooms, changing areas or where staff sleep.

What if something goes missing?

Report it to us within 48 hours, and to the police if you suspect theft. We’ll cooperate fully and assess each case on its facts. Please keep cash, jewellery and documents locked away and never share cards or PINs with staff.

How do you check staff?

Identity and age (18+), police verification, two references, previous work history, a doctor’s fitness certificate and contagious-illness screening — before anyone is sent to your home.

How do I raise a complaint?

Call, WhatsApp or write to us. We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 15 days.

Do staff pay any fee to Movira Care?

Never. We don’t take any fee, deposit or commission from staff, and we don’t keep their original documents.
