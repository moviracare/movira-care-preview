In Jammu, help at home is usually found the same way: a number passed around, someone sent from house to house, and after that, nobody responsible. When it works, it’s luck. Before day one

Before anyone rings your bell.

Every person we send passes the same six checks, and we keep the record.

  1. Check 01

    Identity checked

    Government ID, photograph, address proof, mobile verified by call-back

  2. Check 02

    Age 18 or above

    Proof of age seen and recorded

  3. Check 03

    Police verification

    Character certificate, plus a signed declaration of no criminal case

  4. Check 04

    Two references called

    Previous employers or neighbours, and work history checked

  5. Check 05

    Health screened

    Doctor’s fitness certificate; TB and contagious-illness screening

  6. Check 06

    Trained before day one

    In-house training at our office; nurses bring course certificates and experience

Before you call

Questions

Good to know before you call.

All questions
Are your staff Movira employees?

No. They’re registered with Movira Care. We check and train them, pay them every month, supervise them and replace them if they leave. You pay only us, never the staff member.

Why does Movira cost more than hiring someone directly?

Because the price covers more than the person’s pay: checks and training before they start, their pay on time through us, a supervisor, a monthly home visit and a daily log, a replacement if they leave, and one written agreement. You’ll get a written quote before you commit.

Can I meet the staff member first?

Yes. We share their name, photo and ID card number, and you can meet them at our office in Channi Himmat.

Can I choose a female or male caretaker?

Yes. Tell us your preference when you first contact us.

What if the staff member doesn’t come?

Call us. We aim to send a replacement within 72 hours, and you’re not charged for days no one came.

Should I pay the staff member directly?

No. Please pay only Movira Care, and don’t give wages, advances or loans to staff. Small festival gifts are fine.
