Registration fee

  • Refunded in full if we cannot offer a suitable match within 10 days of registration.
  • Refunded in full if you cancel before we share a profile.
  • Not refundable once you accept a match — our replacement promise applies instead.

Ending the service

Either party may end the agreement with 7 days’ written notice, or by paying 7 days’ service charge in lieu.

If the person receiving care passes away, is admitted to hospital or moves to a care facility, charges stop 3 days after you inform us, with no further notice period.

Final settlement

Any unused prepaid amount is refunded within 7 working days.
