Good care comes from people who are paid on time, properly rested and treated with respect. That is where we start.

What you get

Written down in your agreement.

  • A written employment agreement

    Explained in your language, with the key points in Hindi.

  • Salary by the 5th

    Into your bank account every month, with a payslip. Never below the government minimum wage.

  • Weekly off and proper rest

    Double pay for overtime, and proper rest on 24-hour and live-in duty.

  • Training before your first job

    Hygiene, safe lifting, first aid, safeguarding and privacy.

  • A supervisor to call

    And the right to refuse unsafe or unlawful work.

  • No fee. Ever.

    We never take any fee, deposit or commission from you, and we don’t keep your original documents.

What you’ll need

  • Age 18 or above
  • ID and address proof
  • Police verification
  • A doctor’s fitness certificate
  • Two references
  • Nurses: valid nursing-council registration
