Baby Care

Baby care at home, with rules we never bend

Whether you’re back at work, recovering after delivery or just need an extra pair of hands, we send verified baby care staff who follow your routine — and our child-safety rules, written into your agreement.

Request baby careCall +91 70512 29555

Who it’s for

  • Working parents who need reliable daytime help
  • Mothers recovering after delivery
  • Families with twins or more than one small child

What’s included

  • Feeding
  • Bathing
  • Diapering
  • Sleep routine
  • Play and supervision
  • The child’s laundry
  • School pick-up and dropAuthorised persons only

Our child-safety rules — part of your contract

  • Your child is never left alone, near water, on a raised surface, or with anyone not on your authorised list
  • No physical punishment, shouting, threats or humiliation. Ever.
  • No sedatives or restraint of any kind
  • Medicines only as the doctor prescribed. No home remedies.
  • Your child leaves the house only with your written permission, and is handed over only to people you’ve named
  • No photos, videos or social media posts of your child by our staff
  • Any injury, illness or concern is reported to you and to us at once

What we’ll ask you

Feeding (breast milk, formula, solids), allergies, your child’s doctor, the daily routine, and exactly who is allowed to collect your child.

Duty options

  • 8-hour duty

    Standard shift with one weekly off.

  • 12-hour duty

    8 hours plus 4 hours of overtime, billed at the overtime rate.

  • Live-in

    Stays at your home. Needs a private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

  • Part-time

    Fixed hours each day.

Questions families ask

Can the baby care worker take my child outside?

Only with your written permission, and your child is only ever handed over to the people you have named on the authorised list.

Let’s talk about your little one’s routine.

A short conversation is enough to know what kind of help fits. No obligation.
