Home Cook

Rajma on Sunday, light food for a diabetic parent, Jain meals, or simply dinner ready when you’re home from work. We send verified cooks for part-time or full-day duty.

Who it’s for

  • Working couples and busy families
  • Elderly parents who need regular, simple meals
  • Households following a doctor-advised diet

What’s included

  • Daily cooking to your menu
  • Veg, non-veg, Jain or special diets
  • Utensils and a clean kitchenIf agreed in your schedule

Clear limits

Your cook does only the tasks written in your agreement. Extra work, another address or longer hours need our approval first, so everyone stays clear on what was agreed.

What we’ll ask you

The number of people at home, meals per day, veg / non-veg / Jain / special diet, the kitchen appliances you have, and anything the cook must avoid.

Duty options

  • Part-time

    Fixed hours each day.

  • 8-hour duty

    Standard shift with one weekly off.

  • Live-in

    Stays at your home. Needs a private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

Tell us what your family likes to eat.

A short conversation is enough to know what kind of help fits. No obligation.
