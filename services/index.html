    Elder & Patient Care

    A caretaker for bathing, feeding, walking, medicine reminders and company — for parents who need a steady hand.

  • 02

    Home Nursing

    Registered nurses for injections, wound dressing and other clinical tasks prescribed by a doctor.

  • 03

    Baby Care

    Careful, patient help with feeding, bathing, sleep routines and play, under strict child-safety rules.

  • 04

    Fresh home food the way your family eats — veg, non-veg, Jain or a doctor’s diet.

  • 05

    House Help

    Cleaning, utensils, laundry and ironing, done regularly by someone you know.

Duty options

Duty options

Hours that fit your home.

  • 8-hour duty

    Standard shift, with one weekly off.

  • 12-hour duty

    8 hours plus 4 hours of overtime, billed at the overtime rate.

  • 24-hour care

    Two staff in rotation, or one staff member with at least 8 hours’ rest including uninterrupted sleep.

  • Live-in

    Staff stays at your home. Needs a private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

  • Part-time

    Fixed hours each day, for cooks and house help.

  • Weekly off, covered

    Relief staff for that day at the relief rate — your choice.

Pricing

What does it cost?

Your monthly charge depends on the service, the hours and the care needed. It includes the staff member’s salary and our service. You’ll get a written quote after we understand your needs — before you commit to anything.
