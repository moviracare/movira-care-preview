Hours

Duty options

Hours that fit your home.

  • 8-hour duty

    Standard shift, with one weekly off.

  • 12-hour duty

    8 hours plus 4 hours of overtime. Overtime is billed at twice the hourly rate.

  • 24-hour care

    Two staff in rotation, or one staff member with at least 8 hours’ rest including uninterrupted sleep.

  • Live-in

    Staff stays at your home. Needs a private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

  • Part-time

    Fixed hours each day, for cooks and house help.

  • Weekly off: relief staff

    Someone to cover that day, at an extra charge. Your choice.

Pricing

What does it cost?

Yes, we cost more than hiring someone directly, and we’d rather say so. Your monthly charge includes the staff member’s pay and what we do around it:

  • checks and training before they start
  • their pay on time, by the 5th, so you never handle wages, advances or loans
  • a supervisor at our office, a monthly visit to your home, and a daily log
  • a replacement if they leave, and no charge for days no one came
  • one written agreement and one invoice

The price depends on the service, the hours and the care needed. You’ll get a written quote before you commit to anything.
