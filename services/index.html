Duty options

Duty options

Hours that fit your home.

  • 8-hour duty

    Standard shift, with one weekly off.

  • 12-hour duty

    8 hours plus 4 hours of overtime, billed at the overtime rate.

  • 24-hour care

    Two staff in rotation, or one staff member with at least 8 hours’ rest including uninterrupted sleep.

  • Live-in

    Staff stays at your home. Needs a private place to sleep, meals and washroom access.

  • Part-time

    Fixed hours each day, for cooks and house help.

  • Weekly off, covered

    Relief staff for that day at the relief rate — your choice.

Pricing

What does it *cost*?

Your monthly charge depends on the service, the hours and the care needed. It includes the staff member’s salary and our service. You’ll get a written quote after we understand your needs — before you commit to anything.
