Nine steps

Nine steps

Start to finish.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Call, WhatsApp, walk in or use the form. We fill in a requirement form with you.

  2. 02

    Your consent

    Before any health information, we explain how we protect it and ask your consent.

  3. 03

    Assessment & quote

    The right care level, and a written quote for the registration fee and monthly charge.

  4. 04

    Match

    A verified staff member’s name, photo and ID card number. Meet them at our office.

  5. 05

    Agreement

    Explained in Hindi, Dogri or English: tasks, hours, charges, replacement, notice, refunds.

  6. 06

    Care begins

    Staff get a written Assignment Order: duties, hours, supervisor’s number.

  7. 07

    Every day

    Attendance, meals and care recorded in a daily log you sign monthly.

  8. 08

    Every month

    One invoice from us. Staff are paid by us by the 5th.

  9. 09

    Changes & endings

    7 days’ notice either way; unused advance refunded within 7 working days.

The registration fee, plainly

  • If we can’t offer a suitable match within 10 days, you get the full fee back.
  • If you cancel before we share a profile, you get the full fee back.
  • Once you accept a match, the fee isn’t refundable — our replacement promise applies instead.
