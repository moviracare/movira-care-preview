Before day one

Before deployment

Six checks, every time.

  1. Check 01

    Identity checked

    Government ID, photograph, address proof, mobile verified by call-back

  2. Check 02

    Age 18 or above

    Proof of age seen and recorded

  3. Check 03

    Police verification

    Character certificate, plus a signed declaration of no criminal case

  4. Check 04

    Two references called

    Previous employers or neighbours, and work history checked

  5. Check 05

    Health screened

    Doctor’s fitness certificate; TB and contagious-illness screening

  6. Check 06

    Trained before day one

    Hygiene, safe lifting, first aid, safeguarding, privacy

Our people

Fair to our staff, too

Good care comes from people who are treated well.

Our staff are employed on a written agreement, paid at or above the notified minimum wage by bank transfer by the 5th, get a weekly off and proper rest, are paid double for overtime, and never pay us a fee, deposit or commission. We don’t keep their original documents.

This matters to you too: staff who are paid fairly and on time tend to stay longer.

While they work for you

Supervised, recorded, accountable.

  • A named supervisor

    With scheduled check-in calls.

  • A daily duty log

    Attendance, meals and care, signed by you monthly.

  • Zero tolerance

    For abuse, harassment, violence and discrimination — by anyone.

  • Complaints handled

    Acknowledged within 2 working days; we aim to resolve within 15 days.

  • Your privacy

    Only what’s needed for safe care; ID copies masked; never sold.

  • Replacement promise

    Free in the first 7 days for any reason, and later on resignation, withdrawal or 2+ days’ absence.
